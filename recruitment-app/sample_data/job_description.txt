Backend Software Engineer
NovaLedger Technologies

About the role
We are hiring a Backend Software Engineer to help build and scale the core
services behind our payments platform. You will design APIs, own data
models, and work closely with product and frontend engineers.

Requirements (essential):
- 3+ years of professional software engineering experience
- Strong proficiency in Python
- Experience designing and consuming REST APIs
- Experience with relational databases (e.g. PostgreSQL, MySQL)
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field

Nice to have (preferred):
- Experience with Docker and containerized deployments
- Familiarity with a cloud platform (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- Experience with asynchronous task queues (e.g. Celery, RQ)
- Prior experience mentoring junior engineers

What you'll do:
- Design and ship REST APIs used by our mobile and web clients
- Own the schema and performance of our core PostgreSQL database
- Participate in code review and on-call rotation
- Mentor junior engineers as the team grows
